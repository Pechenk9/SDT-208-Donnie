FAQs

Why do I need at least six donuts?

We bake and pack donuts in boxes of six, so every order starts from one full box. You can mix any flavours.

Why do prices change when I switch branches?

Every Donnie sets its own menu, prices and availability. When you switch, your cart is emptied so you always see the right prices.

How much does customising cost?

Adding more of an ingredient than the recipe has costs its extra price once. Removing something is free, but it doesn't make the donut cheaper.

Do I need an account to order?

No, guests can order too. With an account your name, email and phone are filled in for you at checkout.

Can I get my donuts delivered?

Yes. At checkout choose pickup at your Donnie or delivery, and enter your address.

Can I order for a specific day and time?

Yes. At checkout choose "Pre-order", then pick a day up to a week ahead and a time window. We bake your box to be ready within that window.

Is it a gift?

Add a gift box with a ribbon, birthday candles and a free postcard with your own words at checkout.

What about allergens?

Every donut page lists the allergens of its original recipe and how long it stays fresh. All donuts are made in one kitchen, so traces of nuts, gluten, milk, eggs and soy are possible.
